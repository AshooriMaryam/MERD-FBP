**MERD-FBP-Release**

A diverse and versatile benchmark database (40 MB) for facial beauty score prediction based on rhinoplasty images has been released by the Control and Intelligent Processing Center of Excellence (CIPCE) Lab, University of Tehran. Now accessible to the global research community.

**1. Description**

The MERD dataset comprises 188 images, consisting of pre- and post-rhinoplasty photographs from 94 subjects, sourced from a publicly accessible online website (https://www.sedghplasticsurgery.com/). Its demographic composition includes 65 female and 29 male rhinoplasty subjects from various races and nationalities, with ages ranging from approximately 18 to over 70 years. The dataset encompasses multiple categories, including Ethnic Rhinoplasty, Middle Eastern Rhinoplasty, Latino Rhinoplasty, Asian Rhinoplasty, and African American Rhinoplasty. 

**2. Database Construction**

The MERD collection and preparation process comprises multiple stages, including screening, head rotation correction, annotation, and outlier rating removal.
